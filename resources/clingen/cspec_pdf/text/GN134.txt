Criteria & Strength Specifications

PVS1
Original ACMG
Summary
Null variant (nonsense, frameshift, canonical +/−1 or 2 splice sites, initiation codon, single or multi-exon
deletion) in a gene where loss of function (LOF) is a known mechanism of disease.
Caveats:
• Beware of genes where LOF is not a known disease mechanism (e.g. GFAP, MYH7).
• Use caution interpreting LOF variants at the extreme 3’ end of a gene.
• Use caution with splice variants that are predicted to lead to exon skipping but leave the remainder of the
protein intact.
• Use caution in the presence of multiple transcripts.

Very Strong
Use the modified ClinGen SVI recommended flow chart diagram when assessing LOF variants.
Apply PVS1 at the default (very strong) level for nonsense or frameshift variants that introduce a
premature stop codon between codons 2 and 4557, as nonsense-mediated decay is predicted..
Apply PVS1 to a canonical splice site variant only if SpliceAI predicts an impact on splicing (an
exception is known in another PCD-related gene, DNAH11). At least one SpliceAI score must be greater
than or equal to 0.2 (PMID: 37352859). However, PP3 should not be applied, as this code is mutually
exclusive with PVS1.
Apply PVS1 at the default (very strong) level for canonical splice variants predicted to skip one of the
following exons, which would shift frame if skipped; 3, 4, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 27, 28,

29, 30, 31, 32, 35, 36, 37, 41, 43, 45, 46, 49, 50, 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 57, 62, 63, 66, 67, 68, 69, 72,
73, 78, or 79.
Apply PVS1 at the default (very strong) level for canonical splice variants predicted to skip one of the
following exons, which contain at least one site where a missense variant has been reported
pathogenic; 38, 48, or 60.
Apply PVS1 at the default (very strong) level for canonical splice variants predicted to skip one of the
following exons, which would preserve frame if skipped, but encode one of the AAA regions that make
up the motor domain = 39, 40, 42, 44, 47, 64, 65, or 71.
If a missense, synonymous, or intronic variant outside of the canonical splice sites has a SpliceAI
score greater than or equal to 0.2 and has been confirmed to cause complete or near-complete
disruption of splicing within the mRNA in a study of patient RNA or minigene assay, avoid PP3 and
PS3_Supporting and evaluate PVS1 instead (PMID: 37352859).
Apply PVS1 at the default (very strong) level for initiation codon (start loss) variants, as the second inframe ATG does not occur until codon 81 and it is not clear whether it can function as an alternative
transcription start site.
Apply PVS1 at the default (very strong) level for deletion variants when the truncated/altered region is
out of frame and predicted to trigger NMD (including single-exon deletions of exon 3, 4, 13, 14, 16, 17,
18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 35, 36, 37, 41, 43, 45, 46, 49, 50, 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56,
57, 62, 63, 66, 67, 68, 69, 72, or 73) or critical to protein function (including single exon deletions of
exon 38, 39, 40, 42, 44, 47, 48, 64, 65, or 71, encoding part of an AAA domain, exon 58, 59, 60, or 61,
which encode part of the stalk domain, or exon 78, which contains known pathogenic missense
variants, or exon 1, which contains the start codon and 5'UTR).
Apply PVS1 for duplication variants if the duplication is either proximal in tandem and disrupts frame,
or internal (in tandem or not in tandem, regardless of its effect on frame).

Moderate
Use the modified ClinGen SVI recommended flow chart diagram when assessing LOF variants.
Apply PVS1_Moderate for nonsense or frameshift variants that introduce a premature stop codon
between codons 4558 and 4624, as they are predicted not to result in nonsense-mediated decay, but
rather to cause C-terminal truncation disrupting a region of uncertain function that represents less than
2% of the total length of the protein product.

would preserve frame if skipped; 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15, 19, 25, 26, 33, 34, 58, 59, 61, 70, 74,
75, 76, or 77.
Apply PVS1 at the PVS1_Moderate level for deletion variants when the truncated/altered region has an

7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15, 19, 25, 26, 33, 34, 70, 74, 75, 76, or 77) or out-of-frame but not predicted to
trigger NMD (including single-exon deletions of exon 79).

PS1
Original ACMG
Summary
Same amino acid change as a previously established pathogenic variant regardless of nucleotide change.
Example: Val->Leu caused by either G>C or G>T in the same codon.
Caveat: Beware of changes that impact splicing rather than at the amino acid/protein level.

Strong
This code can be applied to a missense variant with the caveats below:
Apply PS1 for a missense variant if a comparison variant encoding the same amino acid change
has reached a pathogenic classification using the ClinGen Motile Ciliopathy VCEP’s rule
specifications (not just a previous classification in ClinVar).
SpliceAI must be used to ensure that neither the missense variant under evaluation nor the
comparison missense variant is predicted to cause a splicing defect (SpliceAI scores <0.1), or that
both the missense variant under evaluation and the comparison missense variant are predicted to
cause a splicing defect (SpliceAI scores >0.2).
PS1 cannot be applied for a missense variant if any comparison variant causing the same amino
acid change has previously reached a benign or likely benign classification using the ClinGen
Motile Ciliopathy VCEP’s rule specifications (not just a previous classification in ClinVar).
This code can be applied to an intronic or synonymous variant with the caveats below:
Apply PS1 for a variant predicted to disrupt splicing (SpliceAI score ≥0.2, meeting PP3) that is
located outside of the canonical splice donor/acceptor +/-1,2 dinucleotide positions, if a
comparison variant at the same nucleotide position, with the same predicted impact, has been
previously classified pathogenic using the ClinGen Motile Ciliopathy VCEP’s rule specifications
(PMID: 37352859).
Apply PS1 for a variant located within the splice donor/acceptor +/-1,2 dinucleotide positions
(meeting PVS1) if a comparison variant located within the same splice donor/acceptor +/-1,2
dinucleotide has been classified pathogenic using the ClinGen Motile Ciliopathy VCEP’s rule
specifications (PMID: 37352859).

PS2
Original ACMG
Summary
De novo (both maternity and paternity confirmed) in a patient with the disease and no family history.
Note: Confirmation of paternity only is insufficient. Egg donation, surrogate motherhood, errors in embryo
transfer, etc. can contribute to non-maternity.

Very Strong
PS2 is considered when the patient harbors the variant in the de novo state with no family history of disease
and maternity and paternity have been confirmed (for example by trio testing based on exome sequencing /
genome sequencing).
Strength level is determined using ClinGen SVI’s recommended point system (see attached table).
Use the number of points from the first row in Table 1 (“phenotype highly specific for DNAH5”) if the
proband has 6 or more points according to the attached PP4 Table from clinical features consistent with
primary ciliary dyskinesia including confirmation of an outer dynein arm defect by either electron
microscopy or immunofluorescence, and genotyping by whole exome sequencing, next-generation
sequencing-based PCD panel, OR whole genome sequencing, plus copy number variation analysis,
showing absence of biallelic variants from the following PCD loci associated with ODA or ODA + IDA
defects; DNAI1, CCDC103, DNAI2, ODAD4/TTC25, ODAD1/CCDC114, CCDC151/ODAD3, ODAD2/ARMC4,
and DNAH9.
Use the number of points from the second row in Table 1 (“phenotype consistent with DNAH5 but not
highly specific”) if the proband has 6 or more points according to the attached PP4 Table from clinical
features consistent with primary ciliary dyskinesia including confirmation of an outer dynein arm defect
by either electron microscopy or immunofluorescence.
Use the number of points from the third row in Table 1 (“phenotype consistent with DNAH5 but not
highly specific and high genetic heterogeneity”) if the proband has clinical features consistent with
primary ciliary dyskinesia but no confirmation of an outer dynein arm defect by either electron
microscopy or immunofluorescence.
Since DNAH5 is associated with an autosomal recessive mode of inheritance, if a second variant is not
identified in addition to the de novo variant, decrease the strength of evidence by one level, or
consider the possibility to avoid including the proband in PS2 or PM6 altogether.

PS3
Original ACMG
Summary
Well-established in vitro or in vivo functional studies supportive of a damaging effect on the gene or gene
product.
Note: Functional studies that have been validated and shown to be reproducible and robust in a clinical
diagnostic laboratory setting are considered the most well-established.

Strong
Please see the attached file entitled “DNAH5_Functional_Assays” for a description of all functional
assays approved for use to meet the PS3 code.
Animal models with expression of a patient variant can meet PS3 at the default level but have not yet
been found for DNAH5. Models have been identified for other PCD-related genes (for example a
zebrafish rescue experiment with CFAP298 variants in PMID: 24094744, or a CCDC103 variant
dimerization experiment in PMID: 28790179).

PS4
Original ACMG
Summary
The prevalence of the variant in affected individuals is significantly increased compared to the prevalence in
controls.
Note 1: Relative risk (RR) or odds ratio (OR), as obtained from case-control studies, is >5.0 and the
confidence interval around the estimate of RR or OR does not include 1.0. See manuscript for detailed
guidance.
Note 2: In instances of very rare variants where case-control studies may not reach statistical significance,
the prior observation of the variant in multiple unrelated patients with the same phenotype, and its absence
in controls, may be used as moderate level of evidence.

Not Applicable
Comments:

Not applicable, as this code is specific to dominant disorders.

PM1
Original ACMG
Summary
Located in a mutational hot spot and/or critical and well-established functional domain (e.g. active site of an
enzyme) without benign variation.

Moderate
This code is met at the default level (PM1) by a missense, in-frame deletion, or in-frame insertion
variant in residue 1980-1981, 1983-1987, 2005, 2028, or 2030-2033, which make up the ATP-binding
sites (PMID: 32664073) that have been shown to be conserved among axonemal dyneins, and
confirmed to lack known benign missense variation.
PM1 can be combined with PM5 and/or PP3, however, their combined strength is limited to strong. For
example, a variant can meet both PM1 and PP3_Moderate (which together are equivalent to 1 strong
code), while a variant cannot meet PM1 in combination with PP3_Strong.

Summary

Very Strong

BP7
Original ACMG
Summary
A synonymous variant for which splicing prediction algorithms predict no impact to the splice consensus
sequence nor the creation of a new splice site AND the nucleotide is not highly conserved.

Supporting
BP7 is met by a synonymous variant if SpliceAI does not predict a splicing defect (all SpliceAI delta
scores lower than the cutoff of <0.1).
Synonymous variants in the first nucleotide of an exon or the final 3 nucleotides of an exon are not
eligible for BP7, as they are considered part of the splice acceptor or splice donor region. (The
position of a synonymous variant relative to the exon boundaries can be found by searching the
variant at; https://mutalyzer.nl/.)
BP7 is met by an intronic variant if SpliceAI does not predict a splicing defect (all SpliceAI delta scores
lower than the cutoff of <0.1).
Intronic variants between +1 and +6 or between -1 and -20 are not eligible for BP7, as they are
considered part of the splice donor or splice acceptor region (PMID: 37352859).
Although a SpliceAI score <0.1 will also meet BP4, the BP7 and BP4 codes can be used at the same
time, as this is not considered to be double-counting (PMID: 37352859).
