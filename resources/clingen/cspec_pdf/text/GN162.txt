Criteria & Strength Specifications

PVS1
Original ACMG
Summary
Null variant (nonsense, frameshift, canonical +/−1 or 2 splice sites, initiation codon,
single or multi-exon deletion) in a gene where loss of function (LOF) is a known
mechanism of disease.
Caveats:
• Beware of genes where LOF is not a known disease mechanism (e.g. GFAP, MYH7).
• Use caution interpreting LOF variants at the extreme 3’ end of a gene.
• Use caution with splice variants that are predicted to lead to exon skipping but leave

the remainder of the protein intact.
• Use caution in the presence of multiple transcripts.
VCEP
The Ocular Anterior Segment Dysgenesis VCEP will utilize SVI’s PVS1
Specifications:recommendation for determining applicable PVS1 strength level as well as
using experimental splicing data to score non-canonical splice variants
under the PVS1 code (Abou Tayoun et al 2018; PMID:30192042 and Walker
et. al., PMID 37352859).
The Ocular Anterior Segment Dysgenesis VCEP would like to highlight
the reported impact of the PAX6 Downstream Regulatory Region
(DRR) to PAX6 expression. The ClinGen Dosage Curation Working
Group has curated the PAX6 DRR (11p13 PAX6 Downstream
Regulatory Region (within introns 5-7 of ELP4); ISCA-46758) as having
sufficient evidence of haploinsufficiency which is in line with clinical
observations of PAX6-related ocular dysgenesis in cases where the
minimum deletion region chr11:31626701-31644792 (hg38) (PMID
30291432) which includes intron 7 to exon 9 of ELP4 which is
approximately 180kb 3’ of PAX6.
Extension variants will be curated under PVS1 to recognize the
evidence of this variant type leading to PAX6-related ocular
dysgenesis in at least 21 families and shown to segregate with
aniridia in at least 3 families (PMIDs 32360764, 32396632,
29618921, 26661695, 18483559, 22361317, 21850189, 35791194,
36816037)
Splice impacting variants in exons 2 and 3 of the 5' UTR may be
scored under PVS1 if there is a predicted impact to splicing due to
disruption of a canonical splice site OR experimental evidence of
altered splicing OR evidence of reduced expression to ≤50% of
wildtype (See Filatova et. al., PMID 34174135)

Summary

PS4

Original ACMG
Summary
The prevalence of the variant in affected individuals is significantly increased compared to
the prevalence in controls.
Note 1: Relative risk (RR) or odds ratio (OR), as obtained from case-control studies, is
>5.0 and the confidence interval around the estimate of RR or OR does not include 1.0.
See manuscript for detailed guidance.
Note 2: In instances of very rare variants where case-control studies may not reach
statistical significance, the prior observation of the variant in multiple unrelated patients
with the same phenotype, and its absence in controls, may be used as moderate level of
evidence.
